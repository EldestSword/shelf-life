# Cheese research record

Researched 13 September 2026. The game contains original summaries of ten documented dishes or traditional preparations. Source websites are optional links in the Atlas; the game never requests them automatically. The two board tiles are a shorthand for cheese plus prepared ingredients, not complete cooking instructions.

| Stable ID | Preparation and evidence | Source |
|---|---|---|
| `manchego` | Manchego with thinner slices of membrillo; the regulatory council separately supports the Manchega sheep’s-milk description. | [ICEX recipe](https://www.foodswinesfromspain.com/en/fws-academy/recipes/2017/october/cheese-and-quince-paste), [regulatory council](https://www.quesomanchego.es/queso-manchego/) |
| `wensleydale` | Wensleydale and rich fruitcake. A specialist Yorkshire cheesemonger documents the pairing and the cow’s-milk cheese it supplies. | [The Courtyard Dairy](https://www.thecourtyarddairy.co.uk/shop/buy-cheese/cheese-selection-boxes/christmas-cake-cheese/) |
| `raclette` | Melted Raclette du Valais, boiled unpeeled potatoes, pickled onions and gherkins. The regional body supports the raw cow’s-milk and AOP wording. | [Valais tourism](https://www.valais.ch/en/taste/valais-specialties/raclette) |
| `rarebit` | The specified variation uses mature Welsh cheddar, ale and laverbread on toast. The full ingredients follow the official recipe; no origin legend is repeated. | [Visit Wales](https://www.visitwales.com/things-do/food-and-drink/welsh-food-and-recipes/welsh-rarebit-laverbread-and-ale) |
| `aligot` | Fresh tome worked into potato purée with cream, butter and garlic. Mature Laguiole is not presented as interchangeable with fresh tome. | [Rodez tourism office](https://www.rodez-tourisme.fr/inspirations/gastronomie/specialites-aveyron/aligot/) |
| `halloumi` | Halloumi and watermelon as a Cypriot summer breakfast or light supper. No required grilling method, milk blend or protected-status claim is inferred. | [Visit Cyprus](https://www.visitcyprus.com/discover-cyprus/food-drink/local-produce/watermelon/) |
| `khachapuri` | Imeretian: round bread enclosing fresh Imeretian cheese. The regional account supports thin dough and traditional ketsi baking. This entry does not borrow the Adjarian egg-topped boat shape. | [Georgia Travel](https://georgia.travel/get-a-taste-of-khachapuri) |
| `pao` | Ângela’s Minas Gerais preparation: sweet cassava starch, Minas meia cura, eggs, butter, milk and salt. Other published regional variants are acknowledged rather than conflated. | [Minas Gerais tourism](https://www.minasgerais.com.br/pt/blog/artigo/gosto-de-minas-pao-de-queijo) |
| `saganaki` | Kefalotyri is one of the three cheeses allowed by the GNTO recipe. Wet, flour, fry in olive oil and finish with lemon. The brochure is credited to GNTO, 2018, hosted by an exhibition media archive. | [GNTO gastronomy brochure, recipe on final page](https://pub-mediabox-storage.rxweb-prd.com/exhibitor/products/exh-87adacbf-eae4-467d-9fdd-87293a3a0009/product-documents/pro-ba899d49-cdd6-4882-94be-9811a9ba4a11/ed0f8629-2948-4493-aead-45d1dea71f1b.pdf) |
| `fondue` | Equal Gruyère AOP and Vacherin Fribourgeois AOP, garlic, white wine and cornflour. Lower heat before adding Vacherin; do not boil the finished mixture. | [Interprofession du Gruyère](https://www.gruyere.com/recettes/liste-des-recettes/recette/fondue-moitie-moitie) |

Milk type is deliberately absent where not needed or not established by the cited preparation. No ageing ranges, inventor stories or health claims are included. Sources describe a selected preparation, not a guarantee that every regional household uses identical ingredients.
