# Shelf Life: Lou’s Library

## Play

Twenty cells, five columns and four rows. Books belong to Fiction, Ideas & essays, Poetry, History, Natural world or Art & music. Tap one book then another with the exact same section and level to merge. Six tiers run from Single volume to Complete archive. A top-tier archive does not merge again. Acquisitions lets the player choose the incoming section, so strategy does not depend on waiting for a random subject. Tap an empty cell to move; the swap control exchanges unlike items. Dragging is also supported. Return any unwanted item for free to release a cell; higher-tier books and completed dishes ask for confirmation. Undo restores the last board change.

Donations are free and immediate. A pantry delivery needs between two and seven free cells and supplies every named component from the cited preparation. Two different components from the same recipe form a partial preparation; continue adding any missing component until the dish is complete. Components from another recipe and duplicate components are rejected. This is not a cooking simulator or a substitute for the referenced recipe.

Three active requests are always present, paged with arrows. Fulfilment removes the exact book section and tier plus the optional exact dish, gives funds and replaces that request. Twelve recurring visitors have subject preferences and four dialogue stages, advancing after every three completed requests from that person. Requests have no deadline.

## Growth

Start with 60 funds. A book request pays `10 × 2^(tier−1)` plus the recipe value, if applicable. Upgrades add stated donation probabilities, percentage bonuses and flat rewards. The dresser modifies the dish portion before room multipliers; the lamps and atlas add flat amounts afterwards. Percentages are additive. All costs and effects appear before purchase.

Sixteen improvements visibly change the library: trolley, nook, lamps, dresser, conservatory, cabinet, club table, listening alcove, atlas shelves, card catalogue, terrace, bindery, stained glass, periodicals gallery, archive loft and festival. The conservatory unlocks three teal and sea-green palettes. The festival requires all fifteen earlier improvements. Play continues afterwards.

Three visitors in a local calendar day unlock 75 funds, claimable once. Skipping any number of days loses nothing. Eight milestones give one-time bonuses. Discovering each of the ten cheese preparations records it in the Cheese Atlas. The Book Catalogue separately records all 36 section-and-level combinations.

## Word Vault

Two distinct seven-letter target words; nine guesses shared across both. Every accepted guess receives independent feedback for both words. Correct positions are counted first; misplaced matches consume only the remaining copies of that letter. A solved grid freezes, while the other continues. Choose a grid header to view that word’s keyboard evidence. The contrast setting adds symbols to coloured feedback.

There are 49 curated difficult targets and 22,298 accepted guesses. The dictionary is deliberately permissive and includes names and regional variants; the target pool is editorially restricted. Definitions and usage notes appear after every finished puzzle, including losses. No answer clues are exposed during play.

Solve both for 140 funds; solve one by the ninth guess for 30; solve neither for zero. There is no loss of existing funds. Daily puzzles use the local date; practice pairs use a separate seeded sequence and can be replaced without penalty. Rewards are recorded once per completed puzzle.

## Presentation

The recovered screenshots govern composition, palette, typography and navigation. Lou’s coarse original sprite was explicitly rejected. The current art uses a smooth 160 × 200 cel-shaded vector figure with a rounded silhouette, layered brown hair, loose bun, tortoiseshell frames, striped top, teal overshirt and four expressive faces. It resembles a modern illustrated game character rather than a photograph or early-console sprite. Room furnishing is present from the start and becomes denser with purchases. All art ships locally.

Motion uses short tile responses, eased idle and celebration movement and restrained particles. Both the in-game setting and the operating system’s reduced-motion preference are respected. Sound is synthesised locally and off by default.
